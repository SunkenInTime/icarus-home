# Save Valorant lineups in Icarus

> Icarus Strats is a free, open-source VALORANT strategy planner for Windows, with a web beta. It pins lineups right on the map with your standing spot, landing spot, screenshots, notes, and a YouTube clip for each throw.

In the Windows app, lineups save locally to your PC and work offline without an account. Signing in is optional. Strategies you create while signed in or move to the cloud, lineups included, sync across your computers and to the web beta, and teammates can edit them with you live. Signing in does not upload your local library on its own.

## Save a lineup

1. Press G, or click Add Lineup in the sidebar.
2. Drop your agent on the exact spot you throw from.
3. Place the ability where it lands, then press Next.
4. Name the lineup, paste a YouTube link, add quick notes, and paste aim screenshots straight from your clipboard.

## Find lineups on the map

One standing spot often covers multiple default plants, and one choke point can have throws from across the map. Click an agent's spot to see every lineup thrown from there, or click an ability to see every lineup that lands there. Each lineup pulls up its clip, screenshots, and notes.

## Share lineups with a team

Lineups live right inside the strategy they belong to, next to the plays they support. Share the strategy with a link, share code, or `.ica` file and your teammates get every lineup in it. Signed-in teammates can edit a cloud strategy together live with shared cursors showing who is working on what.

## Limits

- Icarus stores the lineups you save and the ones your team shares with you. It is not a public lineup database.
- The desktop app is Windows-only for now. The web beta runs in a browser, requires a sign-in and an internet connection, and works with cloud strategies only.
