Site backdrop

App canvas

The editor background: a finer dot grid on #09090b with a soft radial lift to #18181b at the center.

Torchlight

The torch behind the extras: the same lattice as the sun, lit from one point. Dot size and color both follow the light. The site's own shader, never a gradient.

The sun

The page ends inside the sun, and this is the piece the rest of the brand points at. It is a halftone: a lattice of dots on a 9px cell. Each dot's size and color follow how much light reaches it, so the dark sky is tiny deep-violet dots, the hot center is fat lavender and near-white dots, and where the field cools the dots go silver instead. Scrolling toward the section heats it from 0 to 0.75; the real download finishes it to 1. The same shader lights the app's update dialog.

One rule, drawn ten times: light sets size and color together

Intensity 0.1 to 1.0 on the violet ramp: radius grows with the square root of the light, color climbs #1e0a4a → #7c3aed → #c4b5fd → #f5f3ff, and opacity rises from 35% to full. At rest every cell keeps a faint base dot so the lattice never disappears.

The cool layer

A second noise field drifts through the first on a silver ramp #252530 → #7a7a8a → #e8e7f2. Each dot belongs to whichever field is hotter, so the sun is violet where it burns and silver where it cools. Progress pushes ownership toward violet.

Voice and a slide

Voice

Plain, short, sure of itself.

We talk to a player whose idea is still hot. Feature copy names what the thing does, in the player's words, the way the site does: “One code hands your five-stack the whole strat.” “Fixes ship while the meta is still warm.” “For when the pen isn't enough.” Name the map, the agent, the round. Show the board.

Never: revolutionary, seamless, game-changing, exclamation marks, emoji in headlines, capitals for emphasis, a light-mode version.
